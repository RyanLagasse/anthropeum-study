Vellum Atlas

An alternative look for Anthropeum Study: a printed historical atlas. Light paper ground, dark ink, four printing colours for data. Same six rooms and the same data as the current app.

Plate 1

Shell and Desk

The six rooms sit in a top masthead, so the map gets the full width. The Desk opens on the world as it was in one era.

Plate 2

Atlas

Bubbles are flat ink circles coloured by region. The readout sits on the map like a title cartouche; the ranking reads as a gazetteer column.

Plate 3

Tells

Tells are index cards with a region-coloured top edge. A covered answer is hatched like an engraving, so covered and revealed cards differ at a glance.

Plate 4

When

Each bar starts where a technique first appears and takes that era's colour. Ticks mark arrivals elsewhere, coloured by region. The selected row's rule is set large.

Plate 5

Scripts

Specimens are set like a type foundry's sample sheet: the sample large, then name, dates and the twenty-era strip.

Plate 6

Practice

The photo sits on a pale mount so objects keep their true colour. One red button per state. Calibration figures below are sample values.
